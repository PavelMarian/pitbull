# A6: independent per-column annotation

This packet covers every one of the 179 columns retained in `rel-stack` and
`rel-event` (RelBench 2.1.2 loader semantics).  The two annotators must work
independently and must not have authored the experimental harness.

## Files

- `annotation_items.csv` is the immutable 179-item inventory.
- `annotator_1.csv` and `annotator_2.csv` are independent blank forms.
- `bootstrap_map.csv` is a source-derived candidate, **not a human vote**.  Keep
  it hidden until both forms have been submitted.
- `model_annotation.csv` is a complete conservative model-assisted annotation;
  it is suitable as a provisional operational map but is **not a human vote**.
- `model_annotation_summary.json` summarizes that provisional map.
- `provisional_operational_map.csv` is the compact checker-facing projection;
  its name intentionally preserves the provisional status.
- `agreement.json` and `disagreements.csv` are generated by
  `pilot/a6_adjudicate.py`.
- `manifest.json` records completion state and scope.

## Annotation rule

For each column, assign the earliest defensible time at which its value could
be used without looking into the future.  Judge the stored value, not merely
the existence of its row.  In particular, a row created at time `t` does not
prove that a mutable snapshot column has the same availability time.

Allowed `availability_class` values:

- `row_time`: the table's declared time column is the availability time;
- `column_time`: another named column is the availability time;
- `timeless`: the value is genuinely fixed and available outside event time;
- `unknown`: the data do not contain a defensible availability timestamp;
- `exclude`: the column must not be exposed as a feature.

For `row_time` and `column_time`, fill `availability_column`.  Set `boundary`
to `inclusive`, `exclusive`, or `unknown`; `mutable` to `yes`, `no`, or
`unknown`; `checkability` to `direct`, `proxy`, or `uncheckable`; and
`confidence` to `high`, `medium`, or `low`.  Every row needs a short rationale.

Do not resolve ambiguity by guessing from a column name.  Use `unknown` when a
mutable snapshot has no history, or when an edge has no timestamp.

## Completion and adjudication

After both forms are complete, run:

```powershell
.\.venv-a2\Scripts\python.exe pilot\a6_adjudicate.py
```

The script validates completeness, reports Cohen's kappa for the main class,
reports exact agreement for `(class, column, boundary)`, and creates a sheet
containing only disagreements.  A6 is complete only after those disagreements
are adjudicated and a final per-column map is produced.
